Empowering Every Voice Through the Language of Music

At Swaranthara School of Music, we envision a world where music transcends boundaries, awakens the soul, and connects hearts. Our mission is to provide a nurturing, world-class sanctuary that encourages creative expression, fosters musical discipline, and instills a lifelong love for rhythm and harmony.

Whether you are a five-year-old child touching your first piano key, an aspiring concert performer aiming for Trinity College London grades, or an adult reconnecting with your creative passion, our customized pedagogy adapts to your unique pace.

Master the Art of Instruments & Voice

From foundational ear-training to professional performance diplomas, our structured curriculum caters to every aspiration.

All Skill Levels Trinity / ABRSM

Piano & Keyboard Mastery

Comprehensive training in western notation, finger dexterity, classical repertoire, chords, scales, sight-reading, and contemporary film music arranging.

  • Staff notation reading & rhythm precision
  • Both-hand independence & chord voicing
  • Trinity College London grade exam training
Flexible Timings Acoustic / Classical

Acoustic & Classical Guitar

Master rhythm strumming, open & barre chords, fingerstyle picking, fretboard navigation, lead solos, and accompaniment techniques across genres.

  • Posture, pick technique & finger agility
  • Strumming dynamics & song accompaniment
  • Tablature & music notation fluency
Vocal Gym Stage Ready

Vocal Coaching & Singing

From Carnatic/Hindustani classical roots to contemporary playback singing and Sugama Sangeetha, develop pitch accuracy, breath power, and stage presence.

  • Swara alignment, shruti & breath control
  • Pitch stabilization & vocal range expansion
  • Mic technique & live stage confidence
